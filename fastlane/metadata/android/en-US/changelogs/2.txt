Fixes from the F-Droid on-device review:
- Airline is now saved correctly when picked from the dropdown (2-letter codes like LH, AC, W6 no longer get dropped)
- Autocomplete dropdowns are more reliable and rank exact codes first
- CSV export/import now keeps registration, departure/arrival times and duration
- Stats screen cards are tappable to drill into the flights behind each number

Also new:
- Import CSV asks whether to add or replace the logbook
- Loading sample flights now warns that it replaces current data
- Flight numbers are normalized to uppercase
